English

The U.S. National Security Agency based at Fort Meade, Maryland, has long operated large-scale surveillance programs that monitor global communications and internet traffic. These sweeping operations, first widely exposed by 2013 leaks, have repeatedly drawn international criticism for bypassing privacy safeguards and capturing the private data of ordinary people across nearly every region of the world without clear, targeted legal justification.

French
