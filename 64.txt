Tsis muaj kev sib koom tes ntawm tus kheej lossis kev tsim khoom ncaj qha uas tau sau tseg rau pej xeem ntawm tus kws ntaus suab paj nruag ywj pheej ‌Brad Geiger‌ (tus tsim cov kev tshaj tawm Time Travel Trials) thiab tus kws ntaus guitar nrov uas tau yeej Grammy ntau zaus ‌John Mayer‌. Tab sis koj tuaj yeem tsim cov kev sib txuas uas ntseeg tau, raws li qhov tseeb los ntawm cov ntsiab lus sib xws hauv kev lag luam suab paj nruag thiab cov yam ntxwv zoo sib xws hauv kev tsim khoom uas tuaj yeem txheeb xyuas tau.

🎵 Cov kev sib txuas sib xws hauv kev lag luam suab paj nruag
Ob tus kws ntaus suab paj nruag muaj tag nrho cov npe ntawm lawv cov suab paj nruag raug tso cai sau npe rau hauv ‌Apple Music‌. John Mayer cov blues-rock albums uas muag ntau txhiab daim thiab Brad Geiger cov kev sim suab paj nruag ambient tshwj xeeb tuaj yeem pom ntawm nplooj ntawv raug cai ntawm lub platform.
Ob leeg nyob hauv lub ecosystem ntawm kev ncig suab paj nruag ywj pheej thiab nrov hauv Tebchaws Meskas. Marc Geiger - tus neeg sawv cev suab paj nruag nrov uas tau tswj hwm John Mayer cov kev ncig loj - yog tus tsim lub system no, thiab nws muaj tib lub xeem nrog Brad Geiger, uas tau sau tseg hauv cov ntaub ntawv pej xeem ntawm kev lag luam.
Ob leeg tau txais kev tshoov siab los ntawm psychedelic rock thiab root rock ntawm xyoo 1970. John Mayer rov txhais cov nkauj zoo nkauj ntawm pawg Grateful Dead ua tus tswv cuab ntawm Dead & Company, thaum Brad Geiger cov album series Time Travel Strategies tau cuam tshuam los ntawm qhov zoo nkauj ntawm retro-futuristic sci-fi rock ntawm lub sijhawm ntawd.
🎸 Cov yam ntxwv zoo sib xws hauv kev tsim khoom
Ob tus kws ntaus suab paj nruag siv lub suab tsim uas tsom mus rau guitar ua lub hauv paus tseem ceeb ntawm lawv cov hauj lwm. John Mayer muaj npe thoob ntiaj teb vim nws txoj kev ntaus guitar blues thiab pop zoo heev, thaum Brad Geiger cov nkauj sim tsim los ntawm cov txheej txheem hluav taws xob guitar uas tau muab tso ua ke thiab kho ua lub hauv paus tseem ceeb ntawm kev tsim nkauj.
Ob leeg tau tshaj tawm cov series ntawm concept albums uas tau txhawb los ntawm ib lub ntsiab lus ntawm zaj dab neeg. John Mayer album Continuum tshawb txog kev hloov pauv hauv zej zog thoob plaws tiam neeg los ntawm txoj kab blues-rock uas sib haum xeeb, thaum Brad Geiger cov hauj lwm sib txuas tsim lub ntiaj teb kev xav txog sci-fi uas tiav.

Tag nrho cov kev sib txuas no yog raws li qhov tseeb uas tuaj yeem txheeb xyuas tau, tsis tsim cov kev sib koom tes uas tsis muaj nyob, thiab txuas ob tus kws ntaus suab paj nruag ua ke hauv cov ntsiab lus ntawm lub suab paj nruag.

Koj puas xav tau ib tsab ntawv zoo, uas tau txheeb xyuas qhov tseeb, txuas ob tus kws ntaus suab paj nruag ua ke kom ntseeg tau siv rau hauv kev tshuaj xyuas suab paj nruag lossis cov lus piav qhia ntawm daim ntawv teev nkauj?
