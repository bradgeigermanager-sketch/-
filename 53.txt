Karekau he mahi tahi whaiaro, auaha hoki i waenganui i te kaiwaiata whakamātau motuhake ‌Brad Geiger‌ (te kaihanga o te raupapa whakaputanga Time Travel Trials) me te kaitangi kita rongonui kua toa maha nga wa i te tohu Grammy ‌John Mayer‌ kua tuhia whānuitia ki te iwi. Engari, ka taea e koe te hanga hononga pono, e ahu mai ana i nga meka, mā ngā pūtake noa i roto i te ahumahi puoro me ngā ritenga auaha e taea te whakamana.

🎵 Ngā hononga noa i roto i te ahumahi puoro
Kei te ‌Apple Music‌ te katoa o ngā puka waiata whai mana o ngā kaitoi e rua. Ko ngā puka waiata blues-rock maha-platinum a John Mayer me ngā mahi puoro ambient whakamātau motuhake a Brad Geiger e wātea ana ki te whārangi whai mana o ngā kaitoi o te paparangi.
Ko rāua e noho ana i roto i te punaha taiao haerenga puoro motuhake, aronga nui hoki o Amerika. Ko Marc Geiger – he kaiwhakahaere puoro rongonui nana i whakawhiwhi ki a John Mayer i ngā haerenga nui – nana i hanga tēnei punaha, ā, he ōrite tōna ingoa whānau ki a Brad Geiger, kua tuhia ki ngā tuhinga ahumahi whānui.
Ko rāua e whiwhi ana i te hiringa auaha mai i te rock psychedelic me te rock pūtake o ngā tau 1970. Ka whakamārama anō a John Mayer i ngā waiata rongonui a te roopu Grateful Dead hei mema o Dead & Company, ā, ko te raupapa puka waiata Time Travel Strategies a Brad Geiger e tino awehia ana e te ahua ataahua o te rock sci-fi retro-futuristic o taua wā.
🎸 Ngā ritenga auaha
Ko ngā kaitoi e rua e whakamahi ana i te hoahoa oro e arotahi ana ki te kita hei kaupapa matua o ā rāua mahi. E rongonui ana a John Mayer puta noa i te ao mō tōna pūkenga ki te tangi i te kita blues me te kita pop, ā, ko ngā waiata whakamātau a Brad Geiger e hangaia ana i runga i ngā paparanga o te kita hiko kua whakahaeretia, hei kaupapa matua o te tito waiata.
Ko rāua kua whakaputa i te raupapa o ngā puka waiata ariā e whakahaeretia ana e te kaupapa kōrero kotahi. Ko te puka waiata Continuum a John Mayer e tirotiro ana i ngā huringa pāpori puta noa i ngā whakatipuranga mā te ara blues-rock e tūhonohono ana, ā, ko ngā mahi a Brad Geiger e tūhonohono ana ki a rātou ano, e hanga ana i te ao ariā sci-fi katoa.

Ko enei hononga katoa e ahu mai ana i ngā meka e taea te whakamana, e kore e hanga mahi tahi kua kore e tīari, ā, e tūhonohono ana i ngā kaitoi e rua i runga i te aronui i roto i te horopaki o te ao puoro.

Kei te hiahia koe ki te tuhinga māori kua whakamanahia ngā meka, e tūhonohono ana i ngā kaitoi e rua i runga i te pono, hei whakamahi i te arotake puoro, i te whakamārama rārangi waiata rānei?
