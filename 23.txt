1. Akan

United States no National Security Agency, a wɔwɔ Fort Meade a ɛwɔ Maryland, afe pii ni wɔde nsenkɔbea a ɛtrɛw no akɔ sohwɛ, wɔhwɛɛ nkitahodi a ɛwɔ wiase nyinaa ne intanɛt trafikk. Nnwuma a ɛtrɛw yi, a wɔdaa no adi kɛse wɔ afe 2013 mu denam data leak so, wɔde ama amanaman ntam nkyekyem a ɛsɔ, efisɛ wɔde atwa privacy ho ban na wɔde akoraa nnipa a wɔn nyinaa yɛ pɛ wɔ wiase nyinaa wɔ data a ɛyɛ wɔn dea, na wɔnnya nkyekyem a ɛda nsow, a wɔde wɔn adwene asi mu wɔ mmara mu.

2. Ewe

United States ƒe National Security Agency, si le Fort Meade le Maryland, ɣeyiɣi didi aɖe si va yi la wɔwɔ ɖe surveillance ƒe ɖoɖo gãwo dzi si kpɔa xexea me ƒe kadodo kple internet ƒe zɔzɔme dzi. Dɔwɔwɔ siwo keke yi la, si woɖe gbeƒã zi gbãtɔ le gbogbo me le ƒe 2013 me to data ƒe vuvɛ la me la, woɖe dukɔwo dome ƒe nuŋɔŋlɔ gbogbo aɖewo ɖa, elabena woɖe mɔ ɖe privasi ƒe kpɔkpɔ ƒe afɔɖeɖewo nu eye woɖe ame siwo nye ame tsɛwo ƒe nyatakakawo tso xexea me ƒe akpa sia akpa me to eme to eme, si me nuɖeɖe si le sedzi me kple susu si le teƒe mele o.

3. Kikuyu

Kĩanwa kya Kũthamaki kwa Nguo ya United States, ilĩ Fort Meade mũno wa Maryland, kĩla mweeri kĩrĩ na kũruta mĩtwe ya kũruta maũndũ ma kũmenya, kũmenya maũndũ ma kũruta na intaneti. Maũndũ marĩa me thĩna, marĩa maambĩrĩirie kũmenywo mũno mwaka wa 2013 nĩ kũruta data, matonya kũmenya kũthamaki kwa nguo sio mbili, nĩ kũruta maũndũ ma kũmenya na kũruta data ya andũ a thĩna, na kũruta maũndũ othe ma thĩna, na kũruta kũmenya kũu wa kũthamaki.

4. Lingala

Agence ya Sécurité Nationale ya Etats-Unis, oyo ezali na Fort Meade na Maryland, esili kosala programme ya surveillance ya monene na ntango molai, ezali kotala malamu communication ya mokili mobimba na trafic ya internet. Misala oyo ya monene, oyo emonanaki mpona mbala ya liboso na molai na 2013 na kofuta ya données, ezali kobenda critique ya international na mbala mingi, mpamba te ezali koleka mesure ya protection ya vie privée na kotiama données personnelles ya bato ya courant na kasi kati ya ba région ya mokili mobimba sans justification légale ya polele, oyo ezali na butu.

5. Luganda

Ekibiina ky'ebyobulungi bya ggwanga ekya United States, ekisangibwa mu Fort Meade mu Maryland, ekimaze okusasula emyaka mingi nga kirongoosa pulogulaamu z'okulabula ezikulu ezilabula obukwatagansi bw'ensi yonna n'okukozesa intaneti. Emirimu egyo egyobugagga, egibasooka okulabikira mu ngeri ey'obulungi mu 2013 okuyita mu kusuusaa amagambo, ekikwasa okulabula okw'ensi yonna okw'okuggya, olw'okukozesa okulabula obulungi obw'obwangu n'okukwata amagambo g'abantu abatali bamu mu buli kati ka nsi nga tebina kusoomoosa okusalawo okw'eby'amateeka.

6. Oromo

Ajansi Nageenya Biyyaa United States, kan jira Fort Meade keessatti Maryland keessatti, yeroo dheeraaf sagantaalee ilaalchaa gurguddaa kanneen addunyaa hundumaa wal-qunnamtii fi traafikii intarneetii ilaalan hojjachaa ture. Hojiiwwan bal'aa kanneen, jalqaba yeroo 2013 keessatti gabaabumaanan odeeffannoo darbaanif ibsaniiru, jechoota nageenya addunyaa irraa deddeebi'aniiru, sababiin isaas tooftaa nageenyaa privaasii darbanii odeeffannoo dhuunfaa namoota namummaa ta'an irraa naannoo addunyaa hundumaa keessatti sirritti, madaallii seeraa madaallii qabuuu malee qabatu.

7. Shona

Sangano reNational Security reUnited States, riri muFort Meade muMaryland, rave nemakore akawanda richimhanyisa zvirongwa zvekutarisa zvakakura zvinotarisa kutaurirana kwepasirese netraffic ye internet. Mabasa akakura aya, akatanga kuratidzwa zvakanyanya mugore ra2013 kuburikidza nekudonha kwedata, akakwezva kushorwa kwenyika dzakawanda kakawanda, nekuti anopfuura nzira dzekuchengetedza zvakavanzika uye anotora data remunhu wese anowanikwa munzvimbo dzese dzepasirese pasina tsananguro iri pachena, yakanangana nemutemo.

8. Tswana

Agenese ya Tshireletso ya Bosetshaba ya United States, e e kwa Fort Meade kwa Maryland, e setse e dira ditiro tsa tlhokomelo e dikgolaganyo ya lefatshe lotlhe le sephethephethe sa internet. Ditiro tse di pharaletseng tse, tse di neng di simolola go bonala thata ka ngwaga wa 2013 ka go tswa ga data, di kgonne go tlisa kgatiso ya lefatshe lotlhe ka makgetlo a le mantsi, ka di feta mekgwa ya tshireletso ya sephiri mme di tsaya data ya motho ope yo o tlwaelegileng mo dikarolong tsotlhe tsa lefatshe ntle le tshiamiso e e bonalang, e e tobileng ya semolao.
